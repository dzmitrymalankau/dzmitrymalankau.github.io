A comprehensive safety guide covering physical, social, digital, and emergency scenarios.

## Why This Matters

Most people are trustworthy and the world is fundamentally good. This guide empowers you to **stay in control** by recognizing patterns and knowing your options when situations feel uncomfortable. Knowledge is your best defense—not because danger is everywhere, but because preparation builds confidence and reduces risk.

## Quick Reference Checklist

- [ ] Trust your instincts—if something feels wrong, it probably is
- [ ] Keep trusted adults informed about your plans and whereabouts
- [ ] Verify before trusting online claims or strangers
- [ ] Know at least one emergency contact and your local emergency number
- [ ] Save important contacts and numbers offline (not just phone)

---

## Need Help?

If you're in an unsafe situation right now:

**Crisis Support** (varies by location):
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **National Sexual Assault Hotline**: 1-800-656-4673
- **Cybersecurity/Scam Report**: FBI IC3.gov or IC3.gov
- **Emergency**: Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number

**Tips**: Research and save these numbers before you need them. Trusted adults—parents, school counselors, coaches—are also resources.
